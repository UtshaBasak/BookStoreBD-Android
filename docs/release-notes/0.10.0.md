BookStoreBD for Android 0.10.0 brings the shop's assistant to your phone, with widgets for your home screen and quicker replies to messages.

What is new:

- Ask the shopping assistant, in Bangla or English, about books, delivery, returns or your orders. Open it from the top of Home, from a search that finds nothing, or from Account
- Answers come with the books, orders and pages the shop found, and a button to chat with the seller or the shop when a person is needed
- The assistant is an AI and may make mistakes. Your questions go to the BookStoreBD server, which uses Google's Gemini to answer them, and the conversation is not stored
- Two home-screen widgets: a search box with barcode scanning and your cart, and the shop's current deals
- Reply to a message, or mark it as read, straight from its notification
- The app asks to show notifications when you sign in, so orders and messages reach you from the start
- Share the app with a friend from the Account tab
- Listings, reviews and chat are now checked automatically by the shop, and anything flagged is reviewed by an administrator. Nothing is removed automatically

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
