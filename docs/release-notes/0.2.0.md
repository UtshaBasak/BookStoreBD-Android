BookStoreBD for Android 0.2.0 brings your account and orders to the app, so everything you do as a buyer on the website now works on your phone too.

What is new:

- An account hub with your profile photo and banner, a setup checklist and shortcuts
- Edit your profile, photos, contact details, birthday and password
- Your orders: search, filter by status, sort, and see every book you have bought
- Step-by-step order tracking, cancelling with an optional reason, and returns with photos
- Download any order as a PDF receipt
- Rate and review books and sellers, report a review, and read sellers' replies
- The Wanted board: ask for a book nobody has listed yet, or add your voice to a request
- Home gains Latest books and Top picks for you
- Sort your wishlist seven ways
- Invite friends by email
- Privacy and data: choose what the app remembers, download your data, or delete your account
- Turn two-step sign-in on or off

Also improved:

- Smoother navigation between tabs and screens
- Loading placeholders shaped like the content they stand in for
- Enter on a physical keyboard submits forms and moves between fields

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Next up: notifications and buyer-seller chat.
