BookStoreBD for Android 0.7.0 brings the rest of the website's features to the app.

What is new:

- The home page opens with quick links to every category, new and second-hand books, deals, books in stock and the Wanted board, and a welcome with the shop's promises
- Every shelf on the home page says what it holds; Shop by category shows each subject with its number of books, and Under ৳300 has its own See all
- Book cards show how often a book has been viewed and saved
- Book pages and the cart list cash on delivery, the return window and delivery; the cart offers Continue shopping
- Search results say how many books match and show the sellers whose name matches
- Choose English or Bangla for voice search, and clear your recent searches in one tap
- Notifications can show only the unread ones
- Your account has a buyer side and a seller side, as on the website, with your details and where your sales are paid
- About us, the contact page and the shop's e-mail address are in your account
- Each order in your list shows its totals, copies its number, and can be cancelled or returned from there; an order's page says each book's type, condition and seller

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
