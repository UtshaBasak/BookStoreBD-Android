BookStoreBD for Android 0.6.0 brings the app's shopping in line with the website.

What is new:

- Add a book to the cart, or take it out, straight from any book card, a seller's shop or your wishlist
- A book's page shows when it is in your cart: change how many copies there, or remove it
- A book's page shows how many people have viewed it and saved it, and why you cannot review it when you cannot
- A sold-out book stays in your cart but is left out of the total, so you can check out the rest; ask to hear when it is back from the cart or the wishlist
- Checkout lists your books with their quantities, asks for your e-mail address, and tells you about the promo codes running and how long delivery takes
- The order confirmation shows what you ordered, offers the receipt as a PDF, and takes you to tracking
- A seller's shop can be searched, sorted and filtered to books in stock, and shows how long they have been selling and how their books are reviewed

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
