BookStoreBD for Android 0.4.0 brings selling to the app: list your books, keep them up to date, and handle their orders from your phone.

What is new:

- List a book with up to 10 photos, its details, condition and categories, a price and an optional discount
- See what a book sells for, and what you receive after the 5% fee, as you type
- Have a book someone asked for on the Wanted board? Have it? List it fills the form in
- My books: change price, stock and discount, see views, saves and buyers waiting for a sold-out book, or delete a listing
- Orders for my books: search, filter and sort, and see where each payout stands
- Move an order on up to Shipped, or cancel your books before then
- Download the seller order slip as a PDF
- Notifications about your sales open the right screen

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Next up: barcode scanning, app links and home-screen shortcuts.
