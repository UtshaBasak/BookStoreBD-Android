BookStoreBD for Android 0.5.0 makes the app feel at home on every Android device: scan books, open the shop's links, jump in from the home screen, and enjoy layouts made for tablets and foldables.

What is new:

- Scan a book's ISBN barcode to search for it, or to fill in the ISBN when you list a book. The picture is read on your phone and never stored or sent
- Links to BookStoreBD's books, shops, searches, orders and more now open straight in the app
- Long-press the app's icon for shortcuts to search, scan a book, your cart and your orders
- Tablets, foldables and phones held sideways: the tabs move down the side, conversations and orders open beside their lists, and a book's pictures sit beside its details
- Lists and forms keep a comfortable reading width on large screens

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
