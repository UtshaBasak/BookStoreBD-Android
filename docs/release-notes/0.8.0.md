BookStoreBD for Android 0.8.0 keeps the app in step with the website's latest improvements, and makes many small things more accurate.

What is new:

- Change your account's e-mail address from Edit profile, confirmed with a code sent to the new address
- Accounts made with Google confirm closing the account or turning off two-step sign-in with an e-mailed code
- Closing your account cancels orders that have not started, and says clearly what has to finish first
- Checkout sends your order confirmation to the e-mail address you choose, and a retried order is never placed twice
- The confirmation shows exactly what was ordered and what to pay, and explains any book that was left out or had fewer copies left
- Ordered books leave the cart; sold-out books stay there for when they are back
- The cart and checkout show the total before delivery until a district is chosen, and the cart badge counts the copies ready for checkout
- Orders show where they stand and whether they can be cancelled as the shop decides, with free delivery shown as Free
- Reviews and seller ratings open once an order has been delivered; Write a review appears on delivered books
- Order receipts and seller slips mark books that came back
- A new notification when a book in your cart or wishlist is nearly gone
- Your own listings offer to manage them instead of adding them to the cart
- Listings show only the details the seller filled in, and a new book has no condition
- Deleting a conversation removes it from your chats only; the other person keeps theirs
- Numbers typed with Bangla digits are understood everywhere, and many other small fixes

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
