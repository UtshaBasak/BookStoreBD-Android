The first release of BookStoreBD for Android: browse, search and buy new and second-hand books from your phone, with the same account, cart and orders as the website.

What you can do:
- Browse the home shelves: Quick deals, Trending now, recently viewed, categories and popular writers
- Search in English or Bangla, with suggestions as you type and voice search
- Filter and sort the catalogue, and visit sellers' shops
- See each book's price, discount, stock and condition, and share it
- Keep a wishlist and a cart, synced with the website
- Check out with cash on delivery: delivery details from your profile, the delivery charge for your district, and promo codes
- Sign in with e-mail and password or with Google, create an account, or reset your password
- Choose light or dark, and English or Bangla

Built in:
- Saved screens open without a connection, with a clear note when you are offline
- Sessions are protected by the Android Keystore and a key that never leaves your phone
- No analytics, advertising or tracking
- New versions are offered inside the app, verified, and installed with your confirmation

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Next up: your orders, tracking, cancellations, returns and reviews.
